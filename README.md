# DiskAnalyzer
**DiskAnalyzer** currently is a terminal tool for analyzing the space available on the computer and point you to what take up the most space, what are the old files that you can delete.

## Origin
***DiskAnalyzer*** originated from the challenges [Coding Challenge #145 - Disk Analyser](https://codingchallenges.substack.com/p/coding-challenge-145-disk-analyser?r=gjveo&utm_campaign=post&utm_medium=web) by <ins>JOHN CRICKETT</ins>.
One week before the challenge, I had a space problem during an update, I wanted to update my system but the system told me to free up some space and in that moment I wasn't in the mood of going throught all my system to do and also I didn't know how to do that using a tool, So I went and ask :smile: Claude to do for me.
Then as all good engineer, I asked myself: **_ Can't I create a tool that will do it for me or just remind me when somefiles are already old or big, things like that :thinking: ??.
So by the time the challenge came out, it was just a reason for me to develop it.
